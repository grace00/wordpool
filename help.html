What does Wordpool do?

On sites you choose, it swaps a few ordinary words for harder, SAT- and GRE-level synonyms. A safe place becomes a sanctuary. You meet new vocabulary inside writing you were already reading, in real context. Swapped words carry a dotted underline, so you always know which ones they are.

How does the swapping work?

Every substitution comes from a hand-checked list pairing a common word with a rarer one that means the same thing, so meaning shouldn't drift. Swaps are spaced out — roughly one every few sentences.

  • Hover a word to see the original, without the sentence reflowing.
  • Click it for the definition.
  • Code blocks and anything you can type into are left alone.

Which sites does it run on?

Only the ones you add, via Active on … or Add a site in the popup. Everywhere else it stays idle. Pause everywhere stops all swapping without losing your sites or progress.

How does a word become “learned”?

  • You say so — click a word and choose I knew this.
  • Automatically — switch on auto-marking and a word graduates after however many exposures you set.

Nothing is deleted. Learned words live under the Learned tab and can be switched back on.

What is the pool showing me?

Your list, filling up as you learn: cyan is what you've learned, pale is what's left. Click the water to review learned words, or the pale part to see what's ahead.

Can I add or remove words?

Yes — Add a word on the pool page, or clear a word's checkbox to retire it. Set Words swapped on pages to Difficult only to browse with just the hardest tier.

What happens to my data?

Everything stays in your browser's extension storage. Wordpool has no server — nothing you read is collected or uploaded.
